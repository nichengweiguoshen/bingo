# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the owner of this project, using it as a personal daily execution tool. The use context is short, repeated mobile sessions: start the day, choose a main task, mark small wins, review why work did or did not happen, and keep a lightweight sense of continuity.

## Product Purpose

This product helps turn daily execution into a small game loop. Bingo handles task activation and reward feedback, while the discipline system tracks longer-term baselines and recovery days. Success means the user can open the app, understand today's state quickly, take the next action, and feel enough momentum to continue.

## Brand Personality

Light, playful, rewarding. The product should feel encouraging without becoming childish, clear without feeling strict, and game-like without hiding the practical controls.

## Anti-references

No hard visual restrictions were specified. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, overly serious military-control styling, and decorative effects that make the daily task flow harder to read.

## Design Principles

- Put the next action within immediate reach.
- Make progress visible before asking for more effort.
- Reward completion with tactile, readable feedback.
- Keep review and reset actions available, but visually secondary.
- Preserve the tool's compact mobile rhythm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for readable contrast, comfortable touch targets, clear focus states, and reduced-motion alternatives for animated feedback. The app should remain usable on narrow mobile screens and under system light or dark theme preferences.
